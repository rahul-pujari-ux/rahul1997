Agents (Sequential 4-Step Pipeline):

1️⃣  APPLICANT PROFILE AGENT
   └─ Analyzes applicant background
   └─ Calculates income stability score (0-100)
   └─ Assesses employment risk level
   └─ Provides credit history summary

2️⃣  FINANCIAL RISK AGENT
   └─ Calculates Debt-to-Income ratio
   └─ Evaluates credit score risk
   └─ Assesses loan amount risk
   └─ Detects anomalies

3️⃣  LOAN DECISION AGENT
   └─ Synthesizes all risk factors
   └─ Calculates composite risk score (0-100)
   └─ Determines confidence level
   └─ Generates decision (Approve/Reject/Review)

4️⃣  COMPLIANCE & NOTIFICATION AGENT
   └─ Sends applicant notification
   └─ Logs decision for audit
   └─ Generates case ID
   └─ Records timestamp

Risk Score Algorithm (0-100):
  • Employment Risk:        +10 to +50
  • Credit Risk:            +5 to +60
  • DTI Risk:               +5 to +35
  • Loan Amount Risk:       +5 to +50
  • Income Stability:       -10 (bonus if strong)
  • Age Risk:               +0 to +10
  • Anomalies:              +0 to +30

Decision Thresholds:
  🟢 0-25:    APPROVED     (95% confidence)
  🟢 25-45:   APPROVED     (75% confidence)
  🟡 45-65:   MANUAL REVIEW (80% confidence)
  🔴 65-100:  REJECTED     (85% confidence)
